Long timelapses, without babysitting the phone.

Point your phone at the sky, a garden or a building site and leave it. Time2Timelapse shoots for hours or weeks, keeps time flowing evenly, and saves one video to your gallery when you stop.

Free · No sign-up · Works offline

Made for the long run

Set it up, walk away, come back to a finished video.

1

Hours to weeks

At long intervals the camera switches off between shots and the phone sleeps, so a plugged-in phone can keep going for weeks.

2

Time flows evenly

Every frame shows a fixed moment. If the phone can't keep up, the previous frame repeats — no invented frames — and you get an honest report.

3

No flicker

Exposure, white balance and focus lock once the camera settles. Tap to meter a spot, press and hold to lock what you see.

4

Schedules

Start at sunrise, stop after six hours. The phone waits with the camera off until it's time.

5

Stops safely

Battery and storage limits end the shot cleanly. If the app is ever killed, what was recorded is joined and saved on the next start.

6

Up to 4K

720p, 1080p or 4K at 24, 30 or 60 fps, 16:9 or 4:3. Hardware encoding keeps the phone cool.

Optional: back up to Google Drive and watch from your other devices

For shots that run for days while you are somewhere else.

  • Nothing is lost if something happens to the phone. While a shot is running, finished parts upload to an area of your own Google Drive reserved for this app — on Wi-Fi only.
  • See how it's going. On your other phones or tablets signed in to the same account: status, battery, free space, a day-by-day timeline with thumbnails, and a warning if a camera goes quiet.
  • Preview and export a range. Pick a day or any span, preview it, and save it to the gallery as one video. Deleted gaps get a short black transition.
  • Free up the camera from afar. After exporting, ask the camera to remove the parts it no longer needs. It checks them against your backup first; it never deletes anything on its own.
  • The app has its own trash with 30 days to restore.

Deleting from the backup doesn't delete what's on the phone, and deleting from the phone doesn't delete the backup.

Inside the app

Real screens.

Your footage stays yours

Short answer: we never receive it.

  • No account, no ads. The app works fully offline. Anonymous crash reports and, only if you agree, usage statistics help us fix problems; you can change each one in Settings → Privacy, and they never include your footage.
  • Videos are saved on your phone, in your gallery under Movies/Time2Timelapse.
  • The optional backup goes only to your own Google Drive, into an area reserved for this app that doesn't appear in your Drive file list. We have no server; nothing passes through us.
  • You can remove it any time — from the app, or in Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Delete hidden app data.
